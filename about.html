About Me

My background, research focus, and what I bring to medical imaging AI.

Blessing Asare
PhD Researcher at TU Dublin
Passau, Bavaria, Germany
Medical AI XAI Open Source

I’m a PhD Researcher at TU Dublin. My doctoral research investigates how multimodal ocular imaging can support the identification and evaluation of biomarkers for neurodegenerative disease.

I hold an MSc in Digital Health from Technische Hochschule Deggendorf (THD) and a BSc in Biomedical Engineering from the University of Ghana. I develop interpretable, uncertainty-aware medical AI systems that connect rigorous machine learning with clinically useful, real-world applications.

My work spans the complete ML lifecycle — from raw data ingestion and preprocessing, through architecture design, model training, and explainability integration (Grad-CAM, saliency mapping), to deployment of production-ready clinical AI systems backed by peer-reviewed research.

Beyond research, I teach on Udemy and share tutorials on YouTube as “Data with Pas”, making applied data science and medical AI accessible. I hold 80+ professional certifications spanning IBM, CFI, Coursera, Google, and more.

  • PhD Researcher at TU Dublin — multimodal ocular imaging for neurodegenerative disease biomarkers
  • MSc Digital Health completed 2026 — thesis on hierarchical deep learning for diabetic retinopathy detection with Grad-CAM explainability
  • Published in the Journal of Computational Biophysics and Chemistry (World Scientific)
  • End-to-end medical AI systems in ophthalmology, oncology & neuroimaging
  • Udemy instructor & YouTube educator — “Data with Pas”
  • Dean’s Honour List ×3, University of Ghana
