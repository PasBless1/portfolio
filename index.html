I'm an ML and Data Engineer and Master's student in Digital Health at Technische Hochschule Deggendorf (THD), with an undergraduate background in Biomedical Engineering. I build interpretable, end-to-end deep learning pipelines for medical image analysis.

My work spans the complete ML lifecycle — from raw data ingestion and preprocessing, through architecture design, model training, and explainability integration (Grad-CAM, saliency mapping), to deployment of production-ready clinical AI systems backed by peer-reviewed research.

Beyond research, I teach on Udemy and share tutorials on YouTube as "Data with Pas", making applied data science and medical AI accessible. I hold 79+ professional certifications spanning IBM, CFI, Coursera, Google, and more.
